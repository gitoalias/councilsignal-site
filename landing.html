Brisbane · San Mateo County Hearing scheduled

Baylands Development Agreement — 2,200 homes, 6.5M sq ft commercial

The Planning Commission held a public hearing on the development agreement for the Brisbane Baylands Specific Plan: 2,200 homes, 6.5 million square feet of commercial, office, retail and life-science space, a 500,000 sq ft hotel, a middle school, and 157 acres of open space — with a Final EIR prepared.

Why it matters: A development agreement of this scale locks in a decade-plus pipeline. The phasing decisions made this fall determine which residential and commercial blocks go to bid first.

Woodside · San Mateo County Tracked across 3 meetings

Conservation easement release could unlock estate parcels for ADUs

We followed one property — 1 Montelena Ct — from its first Planning Commission hearing in July through a 2–1 vote recommending easement abandonment, to a final Town Council decision hearing in September. The ask: release an 80-foot conservation easement so the owner can build ADUs.

Why it matters: Woodside almost never releases conservation easements. Approval would green-light the first easement-for-ADU conversion and tee up a formal town-wide process — quietly expanding buildable area on the large estate lots builders already target.
